# Supersworn: Street Level, rules summary

Supersworn is a hack of Ironsworn: Starforged for street-level masked heroes in one corrupt city. All Starforged moves work as written unless changed below.

## Changes from Starforged
- Supply is renamed Resources. It works exactly like supply and is shared between the heroes.
- Heat is a new per-hero track from 0 to 5 (see the Heat move for gaining, losing and effects).
- Legacy tracks are Quests, Bonds and Heroics. Heroics replaces Discoveries. Solved cases pay off through vow milestones.
- The starship is replaced by the Headquarters asset (a humble hideout). Starship modules that make no sense are gone. Medbay, Research Lab and Workshop work on the HQ.
- Perform a Heroic Feat was cut. Rescues and disasters use Begin the Scene (Scene Challenge).
- Make a Journey references in powers mean Undertake an Expedition.

## Power ceiling
- Training and Technology sources are open to anyone.
- Any other source (Mutation, Alien, Animal, Supernatural) allows only one power card, with a cost chosen at creation. The other player can invoke the cost once per session as a complication.
- Flight, Invisibility, Biokinesis, Telekinesis and Clairvoyance are villain-only.
- Healing Factor, Super-Strength, Toughness and Enhanced Senses use their street versions.

## Foes
- Thugs: Troublesome as a group. Two or three unarmed thugs need no roll.
- Pros (hitmen, enforcers, private security): Dangerous.
- Bosses (crime family heads, corrupt officials): Formidable. Usually beaten with a Case, not a fight.
- Villains (costumed criminals): Extreme. Drop one rank after exploiting a weakness or choosing the ground.
- A Boss or Villain who escapes, survives a fight you won on a weak hit, or is locked up becomes a Nemesis with a rank, a scheme clock (6 segments for a Boss, 8 for a Villain) and a status.
- A Nemesis can also be a Connection. When you fight or confront one, add +1 per full box on that connection's progress track (max +3).

## Character creation
1. Pick an archetype or make your own.
2. Choose a Source and one Power, following the power ceiling.
3. Choose a Path.
4. Backstory: how you got your edge, how you pay rent, who knows your secret.
5. Take the HQ card.
6. One more asset (Kit is a good pick).
7. Stats 3, 2, 2, 1, 1.
8. Health 5, Spirit 5, Resources 5 (shared), Momentum +2 (max +10, reset +2), Heat 0.
9. Create a Nemesis together with a scheme clock at 2 filled.
10. Swear an Epic background vow and a Troublesome or Dangerous vow tied to the city right now.

## Default truths
The city is rotten from the top down. Supers are urban legend. There are only a handful of costumed people. Police see vigilantes as criminals. The public is split. Training and Technology are the main sources of power. There is one big prison and one asylum, and villains never stay there long. Most action happens at night. It is still a hopeful place.
